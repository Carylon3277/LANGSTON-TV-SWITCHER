Instructor and Student Instructions

  1. Open this HTML file in a modern web browser. It runs entirely offline.
  2. Program is the source currently on air (red border). Preview is the next source (green border).
  3. Select a source on the Preview bus. Use CUT / TAKE to switch instantly or AUTO DISSOLVE / WIPE to practice transition commands.
  4. In Guided Exercises, follow the cues and read the feedback. In Assessment, enter your name and perform eight cues without assistance.
  5. At the end, download your results as a CSV file and submit it to Canvas, if instructed by your professor.

Suggested classroom exercise

Pair students as Director and Technical Director. The Director reads the cue aloud; the Technical Director operates the Preview bus and transition controls. Exchange roles after four cues.

Instructor note

Camera feeds are simulated labeled sources. No actual cameras, prerecorded videos, internet, subscriptions, or account registration are needed. Assessment scoring occurs locally and is not tamper-proof; use as a formative learning activity rather than a secured exam. Results are not automatically transmitted or saved to Canvas.
